Spike 3: encode a canvas animation and a tone to MP4

A 3 second animation drawn frame by frame (frame index only, never the clock) with a white flash at frame 30 (1.000 s), plus a soft 440 Hz tone with a sharp click at exactly 1.000 s, rendered in an OfflineAudioContext. Mediabunny encodes H.264 video and AAC audio into an MP4 with the index at the front. The page then reads each file back and measures when the flash and the click land, so you can see audio and video alignment without other tools.
